# Plate Scanner Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

Plate Scanner is a private app used only by John Flatley Company's property management team to check vehicles parked at the properties we manage against each property's list of registered resident vehicles. It is distributed as an unlisted app: it does not appear in App Store search or browsing, and only authorized team members can sign in and use it. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how it is protected.

## Information we collect

The app collects only what staff need to identify vehicles and match them to registered residents.

| Information | About whom | How it's collected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| License plate number and issuing state | Vehicles at our properties | Read by the iPhone camera when staff scan a plate | To identify the vehicle and count how often it is seen |
| Date and time of each scan, and the property | Vehicles at our properties | Recorded automatically with each scan | To keep a history of when a vehicle was seen |
| Vehicle color, make and model; status (visitor, flagged); notes | Vehicles at our properties | Entered by staff | To describe the vehicle and record parking issues |
| Resident name, unit number, and registered plate | Residents who register a vehicle | Provided by property management (resident vehicle list) or entered by staff | To recognize registered resident vehicles |
| Staff work email and name | Staff using the app | Microsoft work-account sign-in | To control access and record who made each scan or change |
| Device name | Staff devices | Sent automatically with each scan | To troubleshoot scans |

## What we don't collect

- **No photos or video.** The camera is read live on the phone to recognize plate text. No image is saved to the phone, sent to our servers, or added to the Photos app.
- **No location tracking.** The app does not use GPS or record where a phone is. Scans are tied to the property the staff member selects.
- **No advertising, tracking or analytics.** The app shows no ads, uses no third-party analytics, and does not track anyone across other apps or websites.
- **No sale of information.** We do not sell or rent any information collected by the app.

## How we use it

We use the information only to manage parking at the properties we serve:

- Recognize whether a scanned vehicle belongs to a registered resident.
- See how often, and when, a vehicle has been at a property.
- Record visitor and flagged vehicles, and notes about parking issues.
- Keep the resident vehicle list up to date.
- Control which staff can use the app, and record who made each scan or change.

Authorized staff can export a property's vehicle list as a spreadsheet for property management purposes. We do not use the information for any other purpose.

## Where it's stored and who processes it

The information is stored in John Flatley Company's Microsoft Azure account, in a data center in the United States. It is shared only with the service providers that run the app, and only as needed to run it:

| Provider | What it does | What it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft Azure (Cosmos DB, Azure Functions) | Stores the data and runs the app's server | All information listed above |
| Microsoft Entra ID | Signs staff in with their work account | Staff work email and name |
| Apple (TestFlight / App Store) | Delivers the app to team members' phones (unlisted, by direct link) | Crash reports and feedback that staff choose to send |

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid legal request.

## How long we keep it

- **Scan history** (when and where a vehicle was seen) is kept for 12 months, then deleted.
- **Resident vehicle records** are kept while the resident's vehicle is registered at the property, and removed when property management updates the list or the resident moves out.
- **Vehicle notes and status** are kept while they are needed to manage parking, and deleted with the vehicle's scan history.
- **Scans waiting to upload** are kept on the phone only until the phone is back online, then removed from the phone.

## How we protect it

- **Access limited to staff.** Only members of John Flatley Company's property management team, signed in with their company Microsoft work account, can use the app. Downloading the app alone does not give access. Access ends when a team member's account is disabled or removed from the app.
- **Encrypted in transit.** All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS).
- **Encrypted at rest.** Microsoft Azure encrypts the stored data and its backups.
- **Limited administrative access.** Only a small number of authorized people can access the database directly.
- **Activity records.** Each scan and change records which staff member made it.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information and will respond promptly to any security incident.

**Residents.** You can ask us to:

- Tell you what vehicle information we hold about you.
- Correct your name, unit, or vehicle details.
- Remove a vehicle you no longer park at the property.

Contact your property management office or reese.hu@johnflatleyco.com. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law.

## Children

The app is for use only by John Flatley Company's property management team and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
